# Switzerland

Written for Treename (no earlier open skill covered Switzerland). Facts checked against cantonal archive pages in November 2026; procedures differ by canton, check the cantonal page before giving them as current.

## The key idea: the place of origin (Heimatort / Bürgerort / lieu d'origine / luogo d'attinenza)
Every Swiss citizen is a citizen of a commune, inherited (historically through the father; since 2013 the child takes the place of origin of the parent whose surname it bears). The place of origin is usually NOT the birth place, and it is the key to Swiss records: the commune of origin kept family registers (Familienregister, Bürgerregister) for all its citizens wherever they lived, including emigrants. First find the place of origin.

- **Familiennamenbuch der Schweiz** (Register of Swiss Surnames): lists every surname with its places of origin and the period when citizenship was acquired (before 1800, 19th century, 1901–1962). Available online through the Historical Dictionary of Switzerland (hls-dhs-dss.ch) and in libraries. Best first step for any Swiss surname.
- **Historisches Lexikon der Schweiz / Dictionnaire historique de la Suisse** (hls-dhs-dss.ch): articles on families, communes and parishes, in German, French and Italian.

## Periods and record types
- **Since 1 January 1876**: federal civil registration (Zivilstandsregister / registres de l'état civil). Records are held by the civil registry office (Zivilstandsamt / office de l'état civil) of the place of the event and recorded at the place of origin. The family register at the place of origin is the most useful single source. Since 2004 everything is in the electronic Infostar system.
- **Before 1876**: church books (Pfarrbücher, Kirchenbücher / registres paroissiaux) of the Reformed and Catholic parishes, from the Reformation (16th century) in Reformed areas, after the Council of Trent in Catholic areas. Most are now in the cantonal state archives, many with filmed copies on FamilySearch.
- **Citizenship registers, Burgerbücher, Jahrzeitbücher** (anniversary books), genealogies compiled by communes and burgher corporations (Burgergemeinden), notary and guardianship records in cantonal archives.
- **Emigration**: many cantons kept emigration registers and passport registers; emigrants to the USA, Brazil, Argentina, Russia (Swiss colonies such as Shabo/Chabag in Bessarabia), Ukraine and the Caucasus remained registered at their place of origin.

## Access rules
- Civil registry data less than about 100 years old is protected. Direct-line descendants can request extracts from the civil registry office of the place of the event or of origin; collateral lines (aunts, uncles, cousins) usually need a permit from the cantonal supervisory authority. Example, canton of Zug: freely available at the state archive only for births before 1900, marriages before 1930, deaths before 1960 and family registers before 1929; newer data from the civil registry office. Fees apply.
- Publishing results about other people may require their consent.
- Older records (church books, early registers) are at the cantonal state archive, often free to consult.

## Cantonal archives (examples)
- **Bern**: almost all Bernese church books from the Reformation to 1875 online since 2017 through the State Archive catalogue (query.sta.be.ch, "Kirchenbücher"), except a few parishes (Frutigen, Guttannen, Saanen).
- **Zürich**: Staatsarchiv Zürich, church books and the Bevölkerungsverzeichnisse (population lists, 17th–18th century).
- **Zug**: Staatsarchiv Zug, parish registers, citizenship registers, Jahrzeitbücher.
- **Vaud, Genève, Neuchâtel, Fribourg, Valais**: Archives cantonales; French-language parish and état civil records (see also france-benelux.md for reading French records).
- **Ticino and Graubünden (Italian/Romansh)**: Archivio di Stato del Cantone Ticino; parish records in Italian and Latin.
- Every canton has a "Familienforschung" / "généalogie" page on its archive website: search "Staatsarchiv <canton> Familienforschung".

## Community
- Swiss Society of Genealogical Studies (sgffweb.ch / SGFF) and regional societies: lookup help, publications.
- FamilySearch catalogue "Switzerland, <canton>, <commune>" for filmed church books.

## Requests
Write to the Zivilstandsamt of the place of origin (or event) for a "Auszug aus dem Familienregister" or a civil status extract; give name, date of birth, place of origin and your direct-line relationship with your own documents. For pre-1876 records write to the cantonal state archive. German in German-speaking cantons, French in Romandie, Italian in Ticino.
