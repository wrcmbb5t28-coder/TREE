# United Kingdom, Ireland, USA, Canada, Australia and New Zealand

Written for Treename. Check current procedures on the official sites.

## England and Wales
- Civil registration from 1 July 1837. **FreeBMD** (freebmd.org.uk): free index of births, marriages and deaths by quarter and registration district. The **GRO** (gro.gov.uk) online index adds the mother's maiden name for births and age at death; order certificates or PDF copies there.
- Censuses every 10 years 1841–1921 (1931 destroyed, 1941 not taken); the **1939 Register** (on Ancestry and FindMyPast). FreeCEN for free transcriptions.
- Parish registers of the Church of England from 1538, in county record offices; FreeREG (free transcriptions), indexes on FindMyPast and Ancestry; nonconformist registers at The National Archives.
- **The National Archives** (nationalarchives.gov.uk, Discovery catalogue): military service records, wills before 1858 (PCC), naturalisation, migration. Wills after 1858: probatesearch.service.gov.uk.
- Military: CWGC (war dead), WWI service and pension records (partly burned in 1940), medal cards.
- Newspapers: British Newspaper Archive (paid).

## Scotland
**ScotlandsPeople** (scotlandspeople.gov.uk): statutory registers from 1855 (very detailed: parents' names including the mother's maiden name, marriage date of the parents), Old Parish Registers before 1855, censuses, wills. Pay per view.

## Ireland and Northern Ireland
- Civil registration of births, marriages and deaths from 1864 (non-Catholic marriages from 1845). **irishgenealogy.ie**: free images of historic civil records and many church records.
- **Census of Ireland 1901 and 1911** (National Archives of Ireland, census.nationalarchives.ie), free. Most 19th-century censuses were destroyed in 1922.
- **Catholic parish registers** (registers.nli.ie, National Library of Ireland), images free.
- **Griffith's Valuation** (askaboutireland.ie) and Tithe Applotment Books: land occupiers in the mid-19th century.
- Northern Ireland: GRONI (online civil records), PRONI (public records).

## USA
- Federal censuses 1790–1950 (1890 mostly destroyed); the 1950 census was released in 2022. Free indexes and images on FamilySearch and NARA (archives.gov).
- Vital records are kept by states and counties; start dates vary (often 1880s–1910s); death certificates often give parents' names and birth place.
- Immigration: Ellis Island and other passenger lists, naturalisation papers (declaration of intention often names the home town), WWI and WWII draft registration cards, Social Security records (SSDI, SS-5 applications naming parents).
- Find a Grave, newspapers (Chronicling America, chroniclingamerica.loc.gov, free), city directories.

## Canada
Library and Archives Canada (bac-lac.gc.ca): censuses 1851–1931, passenger lists, immigration, military service files (CEF WWI, digitised). Vital records by province (from the late 19th century); Quebec Catholic registers from the 17th century (Drouin collection; BAnQ).

## Australia and New Zealand
State Births, Deaths and Marriages registries (online historical indexes), **Trove** (trove.nla.gov.au, free newspapers and more), National Archives of Australia (service records WWI digitised), Archives New Zealand, Papers Past (NZ newspapers, free).
