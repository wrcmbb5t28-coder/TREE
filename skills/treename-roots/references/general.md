# General and cross-border sources

Use for every case, and especially for migration, the two world wars and the Holocaust. Portal names are entry points, not proof that a record exists. Check coverage (places, years, record types) before treating an empty result as meaningful.

## Large international collections
- **FamilySearch** (familysearch.org): free, login needed for images. Indexed records plus millions of unindexed microfilm images ("Images" / catalogue by place). Huge for church books of the Russian Empire, Poland, Germany, Switzerland, Italy, Spain, Latin America. Family Tree entries are leads only.
- **Geneanet** (geneanet.org): strong for France, Belgium, Switzerland, Italy; user trees are leads.
- **WikiTree, Geni, MyHeritage, Ancestry**: user trees are leads; check whether a tree cites a record image.
- **Find a Grave** (findagrave.com), **BillionGraves**: gravestone photos and memorials, compiled by volunteers.

## World War I (1914–1918)
- **ICRC prisoners of the First World War** (grandeguerre.icrc.org): index cards of POWs of all armies, including Russians, Austro-Hungarians, Germans, French, Italians.
- Country casualty lists: see the country files (Pamyat geroev Velikoy voyny / gwar.mil.ru for Russia, Verlustlisten for Germany and Austria-Hungary, Mémoire des hommes for France, Albo d'oro for Italy, CWGC for the British Empire).

## World War II and its aftermath
- **Arolsen Archives** (collections.arolsen-archives.org): documents on concentration camp prisoners, forced labourers (including Ostarbeiter from the USSR and Poland), displaced persons (DP) and emigration after 1945. Free online search; inquiries are free for families.
- **Volksbund Deutsche Kriegsgräberfürsorge** (volksbund.de, Gräbersuche): German war dead and also many Soviet POWs buried in Germany.
- **CWGC** (cwgc.org): Commonwealth war dead of both wars.
- **OBD Memorial** and **Pamyat naroda**: Soviet soldiers (see ex-ussr.md).
- **ABMC** (abmc.gov): US war dead buried overseas.

## Jewish family history
- **Yad Vashem Central Database of Shoah Victims' Names** (collections.yadvashem.org): includes Pages of Testimony, often filled by relatives; check who filled the page, it names a living or recently living relative and their relationship.
- **JewishGen** (jewishgen.org): unified search, town finder, Yizkor books, JewishGen Family Finder. Free account.
- **USHMM Holocaust Survivors and Victims Database** (ushmm.org).
- Before civil registration, Jewish births, marriages and deaths in the Russian Empire were kept by crown rabbis in separate books, usually in the same state archives.

## Migration
- **Hamburg passenger lists 1850–1934** (Staatsarchiv Hamburg; index on Ancestry), **Bremen passenger lists** (passagierlisten.de, only partly survived).
- **Ellis Island / Statue of Liberty-Ellis Island Foundation** (heritage.statueofliberty.org): arrivals in New York 1820–1957, free login.
- **Library and Archives Canada** (bac-lac.gc.ca): passenger lists, immigration, census.
- Arrival-country censuses and naturalisation papers often give the exact home village, which unlocks the records of the old country.

## Dates and calendars
- The Russian Empire used the Julian calendar until 1918 (difference 12 days in the 19th century, 13 days 1900–1918). Many Orthodox church books continued old style. Keep both dates as written.
- Ages in censuses, military papers and death records are often rounded: give a birth year range, not a date.
- A baptism date is not the birth date.

## Asking an archive (any country)
A good request is short and contains: full name and variants; event and approximate date; place with the old and modern name and the district; the parish or denomination if known; what is needed (a copy of the record, a certified extract, a search); who is asking and their relationship (descendants usually have more rights); a statement that a paid search is acceptable up to a stated amount; contact address. Attach proof of kinship only when the archive asks for it. Write in the archive's language; English is accepted in most EU national archives.
