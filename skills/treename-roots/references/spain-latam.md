# Spain, Portugal and Latin America

Written for Treename; Portuguese sections follow genealogy-agent-skills by rfsbraz (MIT). Check current procedures on the official sites.

## Spain
**Names**: everyone carries two surnames, father's first surname + mother's first surname (Juan García López). Women keep their surnames after marriage. This makes identification much easier: always search both surnames.
**Record types**
- **Registro Civil**: civil registration from 1871 (some earlier attempts 1841). Records at the Registro Civil of the municipality; now being digitised into a central system.
- **Parish records** (libros sacramentales: bautismos, matrimonios, defunciones) from the Council of Trent (1564), often from the early 1500s; held by the parish or the diocesan archive (archivo diocesano). Marriage files (expedientes matrimoniales) give several generations.
- **Padrones municipales** (local censuses), **Catastro de Ensenada** (1750s, Castile), military records (quintas, hojas de servicio), notarial protocols (protocolos notariales) in provincial historical archives.
- **Civil War and repression (1936–1939 and after)**: Centro Documental de la Memoria Histórica in Salamanca; the PARES portal "Víctimas de la Guerra Civil y represaliados del franquismo"; military archives (Archivo General Militar de Segovia for officers' service files, Guadalajara and Ávila).
**Key online sources**
- **PARES** (pares.cultura.gob.es, formerly pares.mcu.es): portal of the Spanish state archives, including Movimientos Migratorios Iberoamericanos (emigration), Catastro de Ensenada, Civil War sources, Archivo General de Indias.
- **FamilySearch**: huge collection of Spanish parish records and civil registration images.
- Regional archive portals (Catalonia, Basque Country, Galicia, Andalusia, Canary Islands) and diocesan archives with their own digitisation.
- **Biblioteca Virtual de Prensa Histórica** and the Hemeroteca of the Biblioteca Nacional for newspapers.
**Requests**: certificates (certificado literal de nacimiento / matrimonio / defunción) online through the Ministry of Justice electronic office (sede.mjusticia.gob.es) or the Registro Civil; parish certificates (partida de bautismo) from the parish or diocesan archive.

## Portugal
- Civil registration from 1911 (earlier for some groups); parish records from the 16th century, transferred to the district archives.
- **Digitarq** (digitarq.arquivos.pt): district archives with most parish registers online, free; Torre do Tombo (national archive).
- Civil records less than 100 years old from the Conservatória do Registo Civil (justica.gov.pt).

## Latin America (for Spanish-speaking families)
- Catholic parish records are the backbone everywhere; civil registration started between the 1850s and 1880s depending on the country (Mexico 1859, Argentina 1880s by province, Chile 1885, Cuba 1885).
- **FamilySearch** holds the largest collections for Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Colombia, Cuba and others.
- Immigration: Argentina (CEMLA arrival records, Hotel de Inmigrantes), Brazil (Arquivo Nacional, Memorial do Imigrante São Paulo), Uruguay, Cuba. Spanish emigration files in PARES "Movimientos Migratorios Iberoamericanos".
- Many families are of Italian, Spanish, German, Jewish or Middle Eastern origin: follow the migration to the country of origin.
