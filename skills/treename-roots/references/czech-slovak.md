# Czechia, Slovakia and Hungary (Habsburg lands)

Czech part adapted from skill-czech-genealogy by midlan (MIT). For Austria read german-speaking.md, for Galicia poland-baltics.md.

## Czechia (Bohemia, Moravia, Czech Silesia)
- **Parish registers (matriky)**: the official civil record of the Habsburg period (Catholic, Protestant after 1781, Jewish from 1784 with separate books). Older registers are in the regional state archives (SOA), most digitised and free online.
- **Online portals by region**: eBadatelna of SOA Praha (ebadatelna.soapraha.cz) for Central Bohemia; SOA Třeboň for South Bohemia; **Porta fontium** (portafontium.eu) for West Bohemia (Plzeň) with Bavarian archives; SOA Litoměřice for North Bohemia; **ACTA PUBLICA** (actapublica.eu) for Moravia (Brno); Zemský archiv Opava for Silesia; SOA Zámrsk for East Bohemia.
- **Access**: registers are transferred to archives after 100 years for births, 75 years for marriages and deaths; newer records from the registry office (matriční úřad) of the place, for direct relatives ("doslovný výpis" or certificate).
- **Censuses**: 1857, 1869, 1880, 1890, 1900, 1910, 1921 in district archives (SOkA), some online.
- **Military**: the Military Central Archive VÚA (vuapraha.cz): Czechoslovak legionnaires database (WWI), records of fallen soldiers; Austro-Hungarian Verlustlisten via ANNO (see german-speaking.md).
- **Land records** (urbáře, pozemkové knihy, Stabilní katastr maps from the 1820s–1840s: archivnimapy.cuzk.cz).
- **Names**: German, Czech and Latin forms of the same name (Johann / Jan / Joannes; Wenzel / Václav). Village names in German and Czech (Budweis / České Budějovice). Many German-speaking families were expelled in 1945–1946: see german-speaking.md for expellee sources.

## Slovakia
- Parish registers to about 1895 (state civil registration in the Kingdom of Hungary from October 1895). Church registers are in the Slovak National Archives and regional state archives; FamilySearch has filmed and indexed most of them ("Slovakia Church and Synagogue Books").
- Census 1869 (FamilySearch), civil registers from 1895 at the matrika offices.

## Hungary (and the former Kingdom of Hungary: Slovakia, Transcarpathia, Transylvania, Vojvodina, Burgenland, Croatia)
- State civil registration from 1 October 1895; earlier church registers of each denomination (Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran, Greek Catholic, Orthodox, Jewish).
- Hungarian National Archives (mnl.gov.hu), the Hungaricana portal (hungaricana.hu) for digitised archives and maps, Arcanum (adt.arcanum.com, partly paid) for newspapers; FamilySearch for church and civil records.
- Place names in Hungarian, Slovak, German, Romanian, Ukrainian: use a historical gazetteer.
