# Italy, San Marino and Malta

Written for Treename (Italian sections of genealogy-agent-skills by rfsbraz, MIT, were used for the method). Check current procedures on the official sites.

## Which state, which record
Italy was unified 1861–1870 (Venice 1866, Rome 1870, Trento and Trieste 1918). Before that: Kingdom of Sardinia (Piedmont), Lombardy-Venetia (Austria), Papal States, Grand Duchy of Tuscany, Kingdom of the Two Sicilies, Duchies of Parma and Modena. The registering authority depends on the state at the date.

## Record types
- **Stato civile (civil registration)**: Napoleonic period about 1806–1815 in many areas; Restoration civil records in the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies from 1809/1820 to 1865; Italian civil registration from 1866 everywhere (Venice from 1871, Trento-Trieste later). Records: births (nati), marriages (matrimoni, with the important allegati / processetti containing parents' documents), deaths (morti), plus annual indexes.
- **Parish records** (registri parrocchiali): baptisms, marriages, burials since the Council of Trent (1563), plus the **stato delle anime** (household census by the parish priest). Held by the parish or the diocesan archive.
- **Liste di leva and ruoli matricolari** (military conscription and service records), at the Archivio di Stato and the military districts.
- **Anagrafe** (population register) at the commune for the 20th century: family composition and moves.
- **Notarial records** in the Archivio di Stato.

## Key online sources
- **Portale Antenati** (antenati.cultura.gov.it): images of civil registration records from the State Archives, free, browse by archive, commune, record type and year; name index for part of the collection.
- **FamilySearch**: films of civil registers and many parish records, partly indexed.
- **Cadutigrandeguerra / Albo d'oro** (cadutigrandeguerra.it): Italian soldiers who died in WWI.
- **Ellis Island** and Argentine and Brazilian immigration records for emigrants (Italy had huge emigration 1880–1920).
- **Archivi di Stato** websites (one per province) and the national archive system SAN / SIAS catalogues.
- **Cognomix** and similar surname maps: only to narrow the region.

## Names
Women keep their maiden name in all records. Records give the father's name (fu = deceased father: "Giovanni fu Pietro") and often the mother's full maiden name. Latin in parish records (Joannes Baptista = Giovanni Battista). Dialect forms of surnames and first names vary.

## Requests
Recent records (and certificates for citizenship by descent, jure sanguinis) from the **Ufficiale dello Stato Civile** of the commune: ask for an "estratto per riassunto" or "copia integrale" of the birth / marriage / death record; give name, date, parents. Many communes accept e-mail or PEC. Parish and diocesan archives answer by letter; an offering (offerta) is customary. Archivio di Stato: research requests by e-mail.

## San Marino and Malta
San Marino: Archivio di Stato della Repubblica. Malta: Public Registry (civil records since 1863) and Catholic parish registers, many filmed by FamilySearch.
